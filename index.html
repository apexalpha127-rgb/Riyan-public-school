OUR HISTORY

Rooted in Values, Growing in Knowledge

Riyan Public School was founded in 1999 in Babawayil, Ganderbal, with a simple aim: to bring quality education close to home for local families. Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Irshad Hussain Bhat, the school has grown over the years from a modest beginning into one of the area's most trusted institutions — now home to 750 present students, with over 5,500 total students who have enrolled and passed through its classrooms since it began, while still holding on to the values it started with.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide every child in Babawayil with a disciplined, academically rigorous education that builds strong character, critical thinking and a genuine love of learning.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in education across Ganderbal — raising thoughtful, capable young people who carry forward a legacy of wisdom and service to their community.

Co-Curricular & Values

Sports, arts and character education inspired by our community's legacy of wisdom, humility and service.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission process?
Parents can submit the online enquiry form or visit the campus directly to collect admission kits and register their child.
What classes are offered at the school?
We offer academic instruction from Pre-Nursery up to Middle School (Class 8th).
Does the school offer co-curricular and sports activities?
Yes! We offer a range of co-curricular activities, including sports, arts, debates, and creative workshops, ensuring the holistic physical and mental development of every child.
What are the school operating timings?
The school operates during standard daytime morning hours, with specific seasonal timings adjusted according to government and local administration guidelines.
Is school transport facility available?
Yes, safe and reliable transportation facilities covering nearby routes are available for students. You can contact the school office or inquire via WhatsApp for specific route details.
Where is the school located?
Riyan Public School is centrally located in Babawayil, Ganderbal, Jammu & Kashmir, making it easily accessible for families across the local community.
