OUR STORY

Rooted in Values, Growing in Knowledge

Since 1999, we have been a place where academic rigor and moral grounding grow side by side.

Our History & Leadership

Riyan Public School was founded in 1999 in Babawayil, Ganderbal, under the visionary leadership of Mr. Irshad Hussain Bhat, with a simple aim: to bring quality middle school education close to home for local families. Built on values of wisdom and humility, our school has grown into a cornerstone of excellence in the region.

PROGRAMS

Learning at Every Stage

A structured academic pathway from early years through middle school, built to grow with every student.

Pre-Primary (Nursery - KG)

Play-based early learning that builds curiosity, language and motor skills in a warm, nurturing environment.

Primary (Class I - V)

Strong foundations in language, mathematics and science, paired with creative and physical development.

Middle School (Class VI - VIII)

Expanding academic rigor with structured subjects, critical thinking, and robust preparation up to the middle school level.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To provide every child in Babawayil with a disciplined, academically rigorous education up to the middle school level that builds strong character, critical thinking and a genuine love of learning.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in middle school education across Ganderbal — raising thoughtful, capable young people who carry forward a legacy of wisdom and service to their community.
